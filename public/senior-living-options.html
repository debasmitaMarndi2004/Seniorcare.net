Senior Living Options

Understanding your options is the first step to finding the right care. We'll help you navigate the different types of senior living communities to find the perfect fit for your loved one.

Types of Senior Living Communities

Assisted Living

For seniors who need help with daily activities like bathing, dressing, and medication management while maintaining independence.

Best for:

Seniors needing daily assistance but not full-time medical care

Typical cost:

$3,000 - $6,000/month

Key features:
  • 24/7 trained staff
  • Medication management
  • Meal preparation
  • Housekeeping services
  • Social activities

Independent Living

Active adult communities for seniors who are self-sufficient but want a maintenance-free lifestyle with social opportunities.

Best for:

Active seniors who want community living without home maintenance

Typical cost:

$1,500 - $4,000/month

Key features:
  • Private apartments/cottages
  • Community amenities
  • Social programs
  • Optional meal plans
  • Transportation services

Memory Care

Specialized care for those with Alzheimer's, dementia, or other memory-related conditions in a secure environment.

Best for:

Seniors with Alzheimer's, dementia, or memory impairment

Typical cost:

$5,000 - $8,000/month

Key features:
  • Secure environment
  • Specialized staff training
  • Cognitive therapies
  • Structured daily routines
  • Family support programs

Nursing Homes

24-hour skilled nursing care for seniors with complex medical needs or those recovering from surgery or illness.

Best for:

Seniors requiring continuous medical supervision

Typical cost:

$7,000 - $10,000+/month

Key features:
  • 24/7 nursing care
  • Rehabilitation services
  • Medical monitoring
  • Specialized treatments
  • Long-term care options

Continuing Care Retirement Communities (CCRC)

Campus-style communities offering multiple levels of care, allowing residents to age in place as needs change.

Best for:

Seniors planning for long-term care needs with financial stability

Typical cost:

$2,000 - $5,000/month + entrance fee

Key features:
  • Multiple care levels on-site
  • Lifetime care guarantee
  • Resort-style amenities
  • Healthcare coordination
  • Predictable costs
