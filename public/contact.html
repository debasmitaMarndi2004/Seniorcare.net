Frequently Asked Questions

Is your service really free?

Yes! Our service is completely free for families. We're compensated by our partner communities when we help families find the right fit.

How quickly can you help us find care?

We can start helping immediately. Many families find options within 24-48 hours, though the timeline depends on your specific needs and preferences.

Do you only work with certain communities?

We work with thousands of senior living communities and care providers across the country, including both large and small operators.

Can you help with Medicaid applications?

While we don't process Medicaid applications directly, we can connect you with resources and communities that accept Medicaid and help guide you through the process.

What areas do you serve?

We help families find care options across all 50 states. Our local advisors have expertise in communities in your specific area.
