Care Services

From in-home care to specialized medical services, discover the full range of care options available to support your loved one's health, safety, and quality of life.

Types of Care Services

Every family's needs are unique. Explore our comprehensive guide to care services and find the right combination for your situation.

🏠

In-Home Care

Professional caregivers come to your loved one's home to provide assistance with daily activities, allowing them to age in place safely.

Services include:
  • Personal care (bathing, dressing, grooming)
  • Meal preparation and nutrition
  • Medication reminders
  • Light housekeeping
Typical cost:

$20 - $35/hour

💊

Home Health Care

Skilled medical care provided at home by licensed nurses and therapists, often covered by Medicare for qualifying conditions.

Services include:
  • Skilled nursing care
  • Physical therapy
  • Occupational therapy
  • Speech therapy
Typical cost:

$50 - $150/visit (often Medicare-covered)

🌿

Respite Care

Temporary care that gives family caregivers a much-needed break while ensuring their loved one receives quality care.

Services include:
  • Short-term stays at care facilities
  • In-home respite services
  • Adult day program attendance
  • Emergency respite options
Typical cost:

$150 - $400/day

☀️

Adult Day Care

Daytime programs offering socialization, activities, and supervision for seniors who need assistance but live at home.

Services include:
  • Social activities and recreation
  • Nutritious meals and snacks
  • Health monitoring
  • Therapeutic activities
Typical cost:

$70 - $150/day

💜

Hospice Care

Compassionate end-of-life care focused on comfort, dignity, and quality of life for those with terminal illnesses.

Services include:
  • Pain and symptom management
  • Emotional and spiritual support
  • Family counseling
  • Respite for caregivers
Typical cost:

Typically covered by Medicare/Medicaid

📋

Geriatric Care Management

Professional care managers help families navigate the complex healthcare system and coordinate all aspects of senior care.

Services include:
  • Comprehensive assessments
  • Care plan development
  • Coordination with providers
  • Crisis intervention
Typical cost:

$100 - $200/hour
